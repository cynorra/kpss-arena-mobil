## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, tactile, and editorial examination training environment tailored for competitive civil service preparation. Blending the deliberate visual discipline of Apple Fitness, the structured gamification of Brilliant.org, the quiet modular utility of Notion, and the rapid-response operational focus of Kunduz, the interface rejects frivolous AI design trends—specifically eschewing neon blooms, generic dark-mode gold gradients, and low-contrast faux glassmorphism.

The core visual posture is crisp, disciplined, and physical:
- **Quiet Academic Canvas:** Crisp porcelain backdrops host pure white modular surfaces with deliberate, physical hairline boundaries.
- **Physical Micro-Tactility:** Interactive states leverage tight dimensional button depressions, subtle surface shifts, and deliberate pill tabs instead of arbitrary glow outlines.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Strict typographic scale, aggressive headline tracking, and tabular alignment create a rhythm akin to a high-end Swiss broadsheet reimagined as a speed-drill console.
- **Strategic Obsidian Impact:** Concentrated dark obsidian focal blocks are deployed selectively to anchor countdown timers, duel stakes, and high-impact analytics without cluttering the primary white-and-porcelain work surface.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high functional contrast, clear subject hierarchy, and purposeful feedback.

### Core Canvas & Surfacing
- **Canvas Base:** `#F5F6FA` (Warm Porcelain). Sets an expansive, low-fatigue workspace that separates card elevations clearly.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Houses question prompts, flashcards, input fields, and options.
- **Hero Obsidian:** `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Slate Obsidian). Reserved for flagship stat displays, active duel arenas, countdown heroes, and high-stakes banners.
- **Structural Hairlines:** `#E2E8F0`. High-precision, 1px solid borders anchoring cards, dividers, and structural slots.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - High Contrast / Primary: `#0F172A`
  - Secondary / Editorial Muted: `#475569`
  - Tertiary / Hints & Meta: `#94A3B8`

### Primary Functional Accent
- **Electric Royal Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Drives primary actions, verified states, question progression, and selected answers.

### Subject Matter Color Matrix
To facilitate immediate visual scanning during rapid 1v1 duels and flashcard reviews, subjects have dedicated categorical tints:
- **Tarih (History):** `#DC2626`
- **Coğrafya (Geography):** `#059669`
- **Vatandaşlık (Civics):** `#7C3AED`
- **Türkçe (Turkish):** `#2563EB`
- **Matematik (Mathematics):** `#EA580C`
- **Güncel Bilgiler (Current Events):** `#0891B2`

Each subject accent is implemented using high-contrast foreground glyphs and text, paired with a 10% opacity tinted background (`surface-subject-subtle`) for pill badges and tag modules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid information intake under exam constraints.

- **Display & Section Headers (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Uses tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on 700 and 800 weights to generate an athletic, editorial weight similar to modern fitness tracking platforms.
- **Reading Passages & UI Body (Inter):** Neutral, highly legible humanist-grotesque structure built for complex multiple-choice prompts and detailed question solution texts.
- **Tabular Numerals (`fontFeatureSettings: 'tnum' on, 'lnum' on`):** Mandatory for all numeric metrics including exam countdown clocks, live 1v1 duel timers, net score tallies (Doğru/Yanlış/Net), and rank statistics to prevent layout jittering during real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout system is built primarily for a fluid mobile viewport (`max-width: 480px` centered on wide displays) simulating a native mobile web application.

- **Grid Architecture:** A fluid 4-column layout on mobile devices transitioning to an 8-column layout when rendered on tablet viewports. Columns are bound by `gutter: 1rem` and outer canvas padding with `margin: 1rem`.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px base modular unit (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `0.75rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`). 
- **Density Control:** Core exam drill screens utilize tight vertical compaction (`space-sm` between input fields and choices) to eliminate unnecessary vertical scrolling during time-pressured sessions, while stats and dashboard screens utilize `space-xl` for breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical, stacked planar surfaces rather than soft diffused blur effects.

1. **Flat Base Canvas:** `#F5F6FA` provides the foundational zero-elevation floor.
2. **Tactile Structural Cards:** Elevated via a pure `#FFFFFF` fill, bounded by a uniform `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, paired with a layered micro-shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
3. **Hero Obsidian Blocks:** Used for countdown and duel hubs:
   - Deep `#0F172A` background with an internal high-contrast top rim: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12);`
4. **Physical Tactile Buttons (3D Depressed States):** Primary interactive elements have an exaggerated bottom border offset simulating physical depth. When active/pressed, the element translates down vertically by 2px, flattening the shadow offset to create a tactile mechanical snap.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft tactile friendliness with structured editorial discipline:

- **Default Element Radius:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) applies to answer choice containers, input fields, flashcards, and alert panels.
- **Large Component Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`) used for parent content modules, duel arena arenas, hero cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`):** Complete semicircular caps applied exclusively to navigation tab bars, categorical subject chips, timer badges, and state counters.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Tactile Clickables
- **Primary Action (Cobalt):** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. Features a physical simulated base using `box-shadow: 0 3px 0 0 #1D4ED8`. Active state triggers `transform: translateY(2px); box-shadow: 0 1px 0 0 #1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary (Editorial White):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, with `box-shadow: 0 2px 0 0 #CBD5E1`.
- **Duel Duelist Action:** Obsidian button with `#0F172A` background and `0 3px 0 0 #020617` base.

### 2. Answer Choice Selectors
- Modular full-width list items with high-contrast indicator keys (`A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`) in rounded square badges.
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`.
- **Selected State:** Border `1.5px solid #2563EB`, background `#EFF6FF`, choice badge fills with `#2563EB` and text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Validated Correct / Incorrect (During Review):**
  - Correct: Border `1.5px solid #059669`, background `#ECFDF5`.
  - Incorrect: Border `1.5px solid #DC2626`, background `#FEF2F2`.

### 3. Subject Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) containers with padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- Uses subject tokens with 10% opacity tinted fill and solid color text (e.g., Tarih: `#DC2626` text, `#FEE2E2` background).
- Tabular numeral count embedded at the right end of the chip in deep slate.

### 4. Countdown & 1v1 Duel Arena Hero Cards
- Card surface set in Hero Obsidian (`#0F172A`).
- Large tabular display typography (`metric-display`) for live millisecond countdown clocks and score comparisons.
- Split player columns with avatar rings, active ping indicators (`#22C55E`), and real-time score counters.

### 5. Flashcards
- Card ratio of 4:3 with flip transition mechanics.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, layered elevation with deep rounded corners (`rounded-lg`).
- Bottom dock containing tactile rating buttons: "Tekrar Et" (Red tint), "Zor" (Orange tint), and "Kolay" (Green tint).

### 6. Score Calculator (Net Calculation Table)
- Dense list rows alternating between subject indicator, Doğru/Yanlış input spinners, and Net calculations.
- Numeric inputs use `tabular-stats` styling with subtle grey fill (`#F8FAFC`), crisp borders, and bold active rings.

### 7. Crisp Navigation Dock (Pill Bar)
- Floating bottom tab bar with 12px margin from bottom edge.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded to `rounded-full` with subtle downward elevation shadow.
- Active item displays as an electric cobalt capsule with high-contrast icon and label.
